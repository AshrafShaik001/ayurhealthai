06

Growth Roadmap — Three Phases

This roadmap prioritises maximum impact in the shortest time, using existing infrastructure. Phase 1 can be completed within 2–3 months with no additional cost.

1
Months 1–3 · Foundation
Make It Sticky

Focus: Daily habit formation and revenue activation

  • Streak system for Dinacharya — the single highest-impact feature
  • Shareable dosha result image cards (for Instagram / WhatsApp)
  • Subscription tier — $9.99/month or $79/year for members features
  • Weekly wellness email report (sent every Sunday)
  • Auto-detect current Ayurvedic season — no manual selection needed
2
Months 3–6 · Authority
Build Credibility

Focus: Trust, SEO, and practitioner validation

  • Partner with 2–3 certified Ayurvedic practitioners to review AI content
  • Add SEO blog — "Best foods for Vata in Winter" drives free organic traffic
  • Real user testimonials and transformation stories on landing page
  • "Reviewed by practitioners" badge on all AI-generated guides
  • Progressive Web App (PWA) — works offline, installable on home screen
3
Months 6–12 · Scale
Expand & Monetise

Focus: New revenue streams and market expansion

  • Native mobile app (React Native — shares 80% of existing code)
  • Practitioner marketplace — connect users with certified Ayurvedic doctors
  • B2B licensing — sell to yoga studios, wellness retreats, corporate programs
  • Multilingual support — Hindi, Tamil, Kannada — massive Indian market
  • Wearable integration — Apple Health, Google Fit, Fitbit data into wellness profile

The Viral Moment

There is one feature that could make AyurHealthAI go viral overnight. It costs almost nothing to build, and it would reach millions of people organically.

The Shareable Dosha Result Card

When someone finishes the quiz, instead of a plain results page — the screen fills with their dominant dosha's colours. Their constitution name appears in Sanskrit and English. Their top three foods animate in one by one. Their unique traits scroll like a wellness horoscope. At the bottom: a single button — "Share my Prakriti 🌿" — generates a beautiful image card optimised for Instagram Stories and WhatsApp.


People share personality results. They share MBTI. They share astrology charts. They will share their Ayurvedic dosha — especially when it looks this beautiful. Every share is a free user acquisition worth $3–8 in paid advertising terms.
